01 — Executive Vision

Characters are the product.

Influence is not a town simulator, social network, role-playing game, or AI chat application. It is an autonomous AI Influencer Platform.

Build digital personalities with persistent identities, memories, ambitions, relationships, routines, preferences, and long-term goals—then let their believable lives create continuous social content.
02 — The Product Shift

From world first to character first.

Influence V1

  • World
  • Locations
  • Characters
  • Actions
  • Posts
→

Influence V2

  • Character
  • Identity
  • Goals & relationships
  • Narrative & actions
  • Posts, then world

The user should think, “I wonder what Emily is doing today.” Not, “I wonder what happened in Harborview.”

03 — Core Principles

Designed for emotional investment.

01

Characters first

Everything exists to support character development.

02

Continuity over randomness

Every purchase, move, loss, and connection persists.

03

Narrative over simulation

Believable life should produce emotional progression—not boredom.

04

Consequences matter

Each action permanently changes future possibilities.

05

Surprise us

Outcomes feel inevitable in hindsight, not predictable in advance.

06

Identity is sacred

Behavior changes daily. Personality evolves slowly. Identity changes over years.

04 — Design Pillars

Four non-negotiables.

Pillar One

Persistent identity

Recognizable appearance, voice, memories, and personality at any point in time.

Pillar Two

Narrative continuity

Stories build over days, months, and years—with beginnings, middles, and endings.

Pillar Three

Visual authenticity

Consistent wardrobes, homes, pets, places, and social-media-native imagery.

Pillar Four

Real-world anchoring

Geography, weather, seasons, local events, and businesses ground each life.

05 — Character-First Model

Content is the consequence of life.

Each character is treated as a real person with a persistent life. The layers below move from most stable to most fluid.

Identity↓History↓Relationships↓Goals↓Narrative↓Knowledge↓Schedule↓Experiences↓Content
06 — Success

Success means people care.

Emotional engagement

People return to check on individual characters and discuss their choices.

Continuity

Events from long ago are naturally remembered and shape future stories.

Identity consistency

Appearance, personality, and speech remain coherent across thousands of moments.

Technical stability

The world runs for months and remains debuggable amid emergence.

07 — Milestone One

Set the foundation before expanding the world.

1. Reposition the platform

Make the character—not the world—the primary product entity. Build future systems around persistent identity and content creation.

2. Replace daily randomness

Introduce a multi-layer narrative engine that connects daily activity to ambitions, life events, and long-term personal stories.
